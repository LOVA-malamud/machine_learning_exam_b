Q5

n = 10
p = 3
R2 = 0.94

Adjusted R2 formula:

1 - ((1 - R2) * (n - 1)) / (n - p - 1)

1 - ((1 - 0.94) * 9) / (10 - 3 - 1)

= 1 - (0.06 * 9) / 6

= 1 - 0.54 / 6

= 1 - 0.09

= 0.91

answer = 0.91
